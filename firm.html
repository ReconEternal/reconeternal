The four points of the doctrine.

Reviewed quarterly
  1. 01
    Patience over performance.

    An adversary worth defending against is patient. We work at their tempo, not at the tempo of a quarterly report. We will sit on a finding for weeks if releasing it would compromise the engagement.

  2. 02
    Restraint as a discipline.

    We do not exploit beyond what is necessary to demonstrate impact. We do not retain artefacts beyond the engagement. We do not name clients, ever, even after the relationship ends.

  3. 03
    Accuracy before volume.

    One precise finding is worth a hundred speculative ones. Every finding we ship is reproduced by a second operator before it leaves the watchfloor.

  4. 04
    Independence, kept small.

    We are privately held, take no outside investment, and cap our partner count at five. We turn down work that would compromise either constraint.
