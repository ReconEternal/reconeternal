Our methodology

Every engagement follows a structured methodology tailored to your environment — not a generic checklist. We use a combination of manual operator-led testing, adversary simulation, and intelligence-driven analysis.

All findings are independently verified before inclusion in the final report. We never deliver false positives, and we never inflate findings to justify scope expansion.

Every engagement is governed by a mutual NDA signed before any work begins. Client identities, infrastructure details, and finding specifics are never disclosed — including in these case studies. We operate under strict information-security obligations.
