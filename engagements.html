Engagements / case study · anonymised

A pan-European fintech, nine months under watch.

A growth-stage fintech engaged us for continuous reconnaissance after a Series C round. Over nine months we shipped 31 findings — three of which would, in our judgement, have led to a material breach within twelve weeks had they been left in place.

ClientFintech · Pan-EU · 1,200 staff
EngagementContinuous reconnaissance
WindowMar 2025 — Dec 2025
Operators2 partners · 4 leads
Findings shipped31 (3 critical · 9 high)
Selected
findings
Critical

Authentication bypass in mobile onboarding

A race condition between the device-attestation step and the KYC submission permitted a crafted client to skip biometric enrolment entirely. Reachable from any unregistered device. Exploited end-to-end in 14 minutes by our operator.

RE-25-0117
Critical

Vendor SSO trust grants admin to a deprecated portal

A back-office portal believed to be decommissioned was still live behind a vendor SSO. The vendor's tenant was misconfigured to permit any guest invitation to elevate; we obtained an admin session within four hops, none of which involved the client's own systems.

RE-25-0244
High

Sensitive logs reachable via misconfigured CDN rule

A rewrite rule on the public CDN exposed an internal logging endpoint without authentication for a 17-minute window during a deploy. We retained capture of session-bearing cookies for two senior engineers.

RE-25-0312
High

Spear-phish kit modelled on the CFO's signature block

Operators built a credible phishing kit using the CFO's public signature block, internal phrasing harvested from a leaked customer email, and a lookalike domain registered four days prior. Click-through on a controlled test was 19%.

RE-25-0388
Medium

Excess permissions on data-warehouse service account

A reporting service account held write access across all schemas, not the two it required. No exploitation observed; remediated within 48 hours of finding.
