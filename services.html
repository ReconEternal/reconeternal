Services / five offerings

What we do, plainly stated.

Five offerings. Each one runs as a long engagement, billed against an annual retainer, staffed by a named partner and a small operating team. We do not sell tooling, certifications, or one-off audits.

Continuous ReconnaissanceRETAINER · ALWAYS-ON

An always-on red-team feed against your external perimeter, your shadow IT, and your supply chain. Findings shipped as they land — not in a quarterly report. Includes weekly written brief, encrypted finding queue, and a dedicated channel into the watchfloor.
