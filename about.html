FIRM / ABOUT EST. 2019 — THREE PARTNERS — INDEPENDENT & PRIVATELY HELD BERLIN · TORONTO · SINGAPORE

Security shouldn't be a privilege
only enterprises can afford.

RECON eternal was founded in 2019 by three former enterprise security consultants who grew tired of watching clients receive generic, template-driven reports delivered by junior analysts — billed at senior rates.

We went independent because we believed serious security work deserved serious practitioners. Every engagement we take is worked directly by a partner or senior operator. No account managers, no junior hand-offs, no subcontractors.

We are a deliberately small firm. We choose engagements where we can make a real difference, and we turn down work where we cannot. That selectivity is what allows us to maintain the quality standard our clients depend on.

◈

◈

Client education
